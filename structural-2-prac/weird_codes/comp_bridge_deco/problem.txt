ZBazar is upgrading its logistics infrastructure to handle regional fulfillment. Large scale orders are no longer simple individual deliveries; they are managed as a tree hierarchy. For instance, a bulk shipment contains multiple district cargo containers, which in turn contain localized town pallets, down to individual customer packages.The logistics division has two primary challenges that must be addressed simultaneously:Decoupling Shipping Tiers from Tracking (Bridge): ZBazar offers multiple corporate shipping tiers (e.g., Economy Shipping and Priority Express Shipping). The business tier dictating the transit service rules must grow independently from the physical tracking apparatus used during shipment.
Dynamic Special Handling (Decorator): Within a shipment tree, specific cargo segments or individual packages require special physical treatments based on their contents (e.g., Fragile Handling or Cold-Chain Temperature Regulation). These treatments are stackable, optional, and can apply to individual packages or entirely wrapped containers at runtime.Technical RequirementsHierarchical Order Trees (Composite Pattern): Design a unified structural model where an entire cargo container, a single pallet, or an individual delivery package can be treated identically under a uniform interface.
Multi-Dimension Independence (Bridge Pattern): Ensure the business logic governing the shipping tiers is completely decoupled from the low-level physical tracking and fulfillment architecture.Runtime Interventions (Decorator Pattern): Allow operators to dynamically apply special handling modifiers (Fragile, Cold-Chain) in any random stackable combination without modifying the structural code of the cargo components.
